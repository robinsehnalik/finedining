At L'Alchimiste, every seasonal menu begins with a journey into the Bohemian countryside. We work directly with small biodynamic growers, regional fishermen, and forest foragers to source wild garlic, sunchokes, and cold-smoked Arctic char.

Our kitchen unites classical French reduction techniques with open fire, extracting purity and intensity from each ingredient. We invite you to experience the harmony of our signature seven-course tasting journey.
